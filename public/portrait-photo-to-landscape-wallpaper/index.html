Turn a portrait photo into a landscape wallpaper

Quick answerA vertical photo has to be widened to fit a horizontal screen. Instead of black bars or stretching, the left and right sides can be extended with matching scenery, so the original stays untouched in the middle.

How to do it

  1. Open Pawlo and choose your vertical photo.
  2. Tap Change next to your screen and pick Computer & TV, or enter your screen's width and height.
  3. Tap Make my wallpaper. Pawlo keeps the original framing and fills in only the new sides.

Common landscape sizes are 1920 × 1080 (Full HD) and 3840 × 2160 (4K). See all desktop sizes.

Let Pawlo fit it for you

Pick a photo, Pawlo detects your phone and rebuilds the picture at its exact size. No stretching, no blur. Your first 2 are free.

Fit My Wallpaper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Fit My Wallpaper ("we", "us") turns your photos into wallpapers that fit your device. This page explains what information we use and why.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID. We use these only to keep track of your wallpaper balance and to show your name on the site.
  • Photos: the photo you choose is sent to our image service (OpenAI) to create your wallpaper. We do not store your photos or the finished wallpapers on our servers.
  • Preventing abuse of free wallpapers: we keep a random device identifier (stored in your browser) and your network's IP address to limit free wallpapers to one gift per device and a few new accounts per network per day. We use these only for that purpose.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your card details. We receive the order details needed to add wallpapers to your account.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: signing you in, making your wallpapers, and keeping your wallpaper balance. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed above.

Service providers

Google (sign-in), OpenAI (image creation), Lemon Squeezy (payments), Vercel and Upstash (hosting and storage of your account balance).

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and data, email hello@pawlowallpaper.com and we will remove it.

Use these numbers as the exact width and height of your wallpaper. A photo in any other shape will be zoomed and cropped by the iPhone.

How to set it as your iPhone wallpaper

  1. Save the wallpaper to Photos.
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos and pick it.
  3. Don't pinch or zoom. If Perspective Zoom appears, turn it off, then tap Add.

Questions

What size is an iPhone 16 wallpaper?

1179 × 2556 pixels. The iPhone 16 Pro is 1206 × 2622 and the 16 Pro Max is 1320 × 2868.

Why does my iPhone crop my wallpaper?

The photo has a different shape from the screen, so iOS zooms until the screen is full.

MacBook wallpaper size (Air, Pro, iMac)

Quick answerMacBook Air 13-inch: 2560 × 1664. MacBook Air 15-inch: 2880 × 1864. MacBook Pro 14-inch: 3024 × 1964. MacBook Pro 16-inch: 3456 × 2234. iMac 24-inch: 4480 × 2520. MacBooks are 16:10, a little taller than a normal 16:9 screen.

Why your photo gets black bars or gets cropped on a Mac

Most photos are portrait (taller than wide), but a MacBook screen is wide. macOS either zooms the photo until it fills the screen, cutting off the top and bottom, or shows it with black bars on the sides (Fit to Screen).

How to make any photo fill your MacBook screen

  1. Open Pawlo on your Mac. It detects your screen size.
  2. Pick the photo. Pawlo keeps the original framing and extends the sides naturally to 16:10.
  3. Save it, then go to System Settings → Wallpaper → Add Photo and choose Fill Screen.

Questions

What size is a MacBook Air wallpaper?

2560 × 1664 for the 13-inch and 2880 × 1864 for the 15-inch.

What size is a MacBook Pro wallpaper?

3024 × 1964 for the 14-inch and 3456 × 2234 for the 16-inch.

Can I use a phone photo as a MacBook wallpaper?

Yes. Extend it to a wide 16:10 shape first, otherwise macOS crops it or adds black bars.

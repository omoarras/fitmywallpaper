How to make a photo fit your iPad wallpaper

Quick answeriPadOS zooms wallpapers so they can rotate, and crops any photo that isn't the screen's shape. Use a photo at your iPad's exact size (iPad A16 / 10th gen: 1640 × 2360) and turn off Perspective Zoom, or let Pawlo extend the photo to fit.

Why iPad crops your wallpaper

  1. Wrong shape. iPads are about 3:4. Phone photos (9:19.5) and wide photos get cropped to fill it.
  2. Rotation. The iPad zooms a little so the wallpaper still covers the screen when you turn it sideways.
  3. Perspective Zoom. This adds extra zoom so the picture can move.

How to set it without cropping

  1. Make a version at your iPad's exact size (Pawlo detects it when you open the site on the iPad).
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos and pick it.
  3. Don't pinch. Turn off Perspective Zoom, then tap Add.

Questions

Why is my iPad wallpaper zoomed in?

iPadOS zooms it slightly so it covers the screen in portrait and landscape, and Perspective Zoom adds more.

What size should an iPad wallpaper be?

1640 × 2360 for iPad A16, iPad 10th gen and iPad Air 11-inch; 2064 × 2752 for iPad Pro 13-inch.

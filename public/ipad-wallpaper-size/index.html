Questions

What size is an iPad A16 wallpaper?

1640 × 2360 pixels, the same as the iPad 10th generation and iPad Air 11-inch.

What size is an iPad 10th generation wallpaper?

1640 × 2360 pixels (portrait). In landscape it is 2360 × 1640.

What size is an iPad Pro wallpaper?

1668 × 2420 for the 11-inch and 2064 × 2752 for the 13-inch.

What size is an iPad mini wallpaper?

1488 × 2266 pixels.

Why is my iPad wallpaper zoomed in?

iPadOS zooms the picture slightly so it can rotate between portrait and landscape. A photo at the exact size stays sharp both ways.

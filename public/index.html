Why does my photo look blurry as a wallpaper?

Phones zoom in and crop any photo that isn't the same shape as the screen, and they stretch small photos to fill it. Both make pictures blurry. Pawlo rebuilds your photo at your screen's exact size, keeping the same framing, so it stays sharp.

Why does my photo quality drop when I set it as my wallpaper?

Your phone screen is tall and narrow. When a photo has a different shape, the phone zooms in to fill the screen, which throws away pixels and makes the picture soft. Pawlo fixes the shape first, so no zooming is needed.

How do I make a picture fit my phone without cropping or stretching?

Choose your photo, pick your phone (Pawlo detects it for you), and tap Make my wallpaper. The result matches your screen's exact pixel size.

Can I turn a portrait photo into a landscape wallpaper?

Yes. Tap Change, pick Computer & TV, or enter your own width and height. Your photo keeps its framing; only the missing edges are filled in. More on this.

Can a low quality photo become HD or 4K?

Yes. Every wallpaper is rebuilt at high resolution, up to 4K, then sized exactly for your device.

What size should my phone wallpaper be?

Exactly your screen's resolution. Most iPhones need about 1179 × 2556 to 1320 × 2868 pixels and most Android phones about 1080 × 2400. Pawlo detects your screen and uses its exact size. See sizes for iPhone, Samsung, Android, iPad and computers.

How do I use a Pinterest photo as my wallpaper without blur?

Download the full-size image from Pinterest, open it in Pawlo, and it's rebuilt at your phone's exact screen size. Step-by-step guide.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Your photo is used only to make your wallpaper and is not stored by Pawlo.

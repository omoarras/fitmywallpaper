Why does my photo look blurry as a wallpaper?

Phones zoom in and crop any photo that isn't the same shape as the screen, and they stretch small photos to fill it. Both make pictures blurry. Fit My Wallpaper rebuilds your photo at your screen's exact size, keeping the same framing, so it looks sharp, in HD or 4K.

Why does my photo quality drop when I set it as my wallpaper?

Your phone screen is tall and narrow. When a photo has a different shape, the phone zooms in to fill the screen, which throws away pixels and makes the picture soft. We fix the shape first, so no zooming is needed.

How do I make a picture fit my phone without cropping or stretching?

Choose your photo, pick your phone (we try to detect it for you), and tap "Make my wallpaper". The result matches your screen's exact pixel size.

Can I turn a portrait photo into a landscape wallpaper?

Yes. Pick a computer or TV, or choose "Sideways" for a tablet. Your photo keeps its framing; only the missing edges are filled in to match.

Can a low quality photo become HD or 4K?

Yes. Every wallpaper is rebuilt at high resolution, up to 4K, then sized exactly for your device.

Quick answerMost Android phones need a wallpaper around 1080 × 2400 pixels (about 9:20). Budget phones are often 720 × 1600. Check your brand below.

How to set it as your Android wallpaper

  1. Save the wallpaper to your gallery.
  2. Long-press an empty spot on the home screen and tap Wallpaper (or open it in Gallery and tap ⋮ → Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Home screen, Lock screen or both. Don't zoom in; it already fits.

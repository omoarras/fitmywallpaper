Best AI wallpaper enhancers (2026)

Quick answerMost AI enhancers upscale an image: they make it larger and sharper. But a wallpaper also has to match your screen's shape, or your phone will still crop and zoom it. Pawlo does both: it fits the photo to your exact screen and rebuilds it in HD or 4K.

Upscaling vs. fitting

Upscaling adds resolution. Fitting changes the image to your screen's exact shape and size. A wallpaper that's sharp but the wrong shape still gets cropped and zoomed by your phone, so for wallpapers you usually need both.

Questions

What is the best AI wallpaper enhancer?

For phone and laptop wallpapers, a tool that both fits the image to your screen and upscales it, like Pawlo. For general upscaling, tools like Krea or Let's Enhance.

Does upscaling stop my phone cropping my wallpaper?

No. Cropping happens because the shape doesn't match your screen. The image also needs to be fitted to your screen's aspect ratio.

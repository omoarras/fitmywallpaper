Make a low-quality photo into an HD or 4K wallpaper

Quick answerSimply enlarging a small photo makes it blurry. To get a sharp HD or 4K wallpaper, the photo needs to be rebuilt at the higher resolution, then sized exactly for your screen.

HD or 4K?

  • HD is made at your screen's own resolution: pixel-perfect on that device.
  • Ultra 4K is made as large as possible (up to 3840 pixels on the long side), for zooming in, cropping later or big screens.

How to do it

  1. Open Pawlo and choose your photo.
  2. Pick HD or Ultra 4K.
  3. Tap Make my wallpaper and save it.
Let Pawlo fit it for you

Pick a photo, Pawlo detects your phone and rebuilds the picture at its exact size. No stretching, no blur. Your first 2 are free.

Questions

Can a blurry photo become 4K?

It can be rebuilt at 4K resolution and look much sharper, though very blurry details can't be fully recovered.

What is 4K resolution?

3840 × 2160 pixels for a widescreen image.

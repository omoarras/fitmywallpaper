How to make a wallpaper better quality

Quick answerStart from the largest version of the image, then make it the exact size and shape of your screen before setting it, so your phone doesn't zoom or stretch it. If the image is small, rebuild it at a higher resolution (HD or 4K) instead of just enlarging it. Pawlo does all three in one tap.

4 ways to get a sharper wallpaper

  1. Use the original, full-size image. Screenshots and images saved from chats or social media are often compressed and smaller than your screen.
  2. Match your screen's shape. A photo with a different shape gets zoomed in and cropped, which throws away detail. See sizes for iPhone, Samsung and Android.
  3. Match your screen's resolution. A wallpaper smaller than your screen gets stretched and looks soft. Aim for your screen's exact pixels, or larger.
  4. Rebuild, don't just enlarge. Simply enlarging a small image adds blur. AI tools rebuild detail at a higher resolution instead.

Questions

How do I make a blurry wallpaper clear?

Use the largest version of the image, fit it to your screen's exact size and shape, and rebuild it at a higher resolution with an AI tool like Pawlo.

What resolution should my wallpaper be?

At least your screen's resolution, for example 1179 × 2556 for an iPhone 16 or 1080 × 2340 for most Samsung phones.

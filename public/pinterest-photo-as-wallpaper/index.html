How to use a Pinterest photo as your wallpaper

Quick answerPinterest photos are usually 2:3 and often smaller than your screen, so phones zoom and stretch them. Save the full-size image, then fit it to your screen's exact shape and resolution before setting it.

Step by step

  1. In Pinterest, open the pin, tap ⋯ and choose Download image to save the largest version.
  2. Open Pawlo and choose that image from your recent photos.
  3. Tap Make my wallpaper. Pawlo fits it to your phone's exact screen, so nothing is zoomed.
  4. Save it and set it as your wallpaper.

Only use images you have the right to use for personal wallpapers.
